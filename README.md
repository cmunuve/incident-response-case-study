# Incident response investigation
This project simulates a small-scale security incident and documents the investigation, analysis, and response process from a SOC perspective.
## Scenario overview
A user reports that their system has become unusually slow and unresponsive. Suspicious activity is suspected and an investigation is initiated to determine the cause.
This case study documents the investigative process, evidence reviewed and response decisions.
## Investigation Workflow
The investigation followed a structured incident response process:
1. **Investigation plan** – Defined the scope and evidence sources.
2. **Timeline reconstruction** – Organized events based on available logs.
3. **Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)** – Identified artifacts suggesting potential malicious activity.
4. **Analysis and validation** – Evaluated alternative explanations for the observed events.
5. **Containment and response** – Recommended actions to reduce potential risk.
6. **Incident summary** – Documented findings and current assessment.
## Key Takeaways
This case study reinforced the importance of correlating multiple artifacts before concluding that malicious activity occurred.  
Individual events such as failed logins or new processes may have benign explanations, but analyzing them within a timeline helps identify patterns that may require deeper investigation.
